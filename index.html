Strength & Conditioning

Training Plan

3 lifting days · 3 easy cardio/accessory days · 1 rest. Tap today's session.
Ground rules ›
  • Exhale on the effort — never hold your breath under load.
  • Leave 2–3 reps in reserve. Don't train to failure (especially pull-ups).
  • Warm up and cool down gradually; stand up slowly between sets.
  • Stop and sit down if your heart races/skips, or you feel dizzy or breathless.
  • Keep cardio easy and conversational — no intervals.
  • No stimulant pre-workouts.
